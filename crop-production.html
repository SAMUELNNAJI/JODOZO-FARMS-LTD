High-Yield, Mechanised Crop Farming

Our crop division produces maize, soybean, rice, vegetables and staple crops across hundreds of hectares. Every field is prepped by our own tractors, irrigated from our own dams, and managed with modern agronomy — soil testing, certified seed, precision planting and timely crop protection.

  • Own machinery fleet for land preparation to harvest
  • Irrigation from farm dams and boreholes
  • On-site storage, preservation and processing
