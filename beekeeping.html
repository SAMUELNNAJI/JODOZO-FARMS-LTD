Overview

Sweet Value From Nature

Our apiaries host modern frame hives sited near our orchards and flowering fields. Bees pollinate our crops while producing premium honey that we extract, filter and package hygienically. We also train new beekeepers through the Jodozo Farm Academy.

  • Modern frame and top-bar hives
  • Unadulterated, single-origin honey
  • Pollination services for orchards and seed farms
